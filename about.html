THE COMPANY

We build the workforce that works while you imagine.

AImagineThat was founded on a simple conviction: the most valuable thing a business owns is its people's imagination — and everything else should be automated.

OUR MISSION

Give every business an autonomous AI workforce.

Most companies lose revenue not from lack of demand, but from slow responses, missed follow-ups and manual busywork. Our mission is to eliminate that loss — by deploying agentic AI that perceives, plans, acts and verifies across every customer touchpoint.

  • Answer every lead in under 30 seconds, on every channel
  • Automate the repetitive 80% so people do the judgment 20%
  • Make enterprise-grade AI accessible to teams of any size
  • Keep humans in command — agents escalate, never overreach

OUR VISION

A world where you imagine outcomes — and agents deliver them.

We see a near future where "did anyone follow up?" is a question no one asks. Where businesses describe an outcome in plain language, and a network of accountable AI agents plans the work, does the work, and proves the work — end to end.

  • Every business operates 24/7 in every language and time zone
  • Software you instruct, not software you operate
  • Verified autonomy: every agent action observable and auditable
  • Imagination becomes the only bottleneck — as it should be

WHAT WE STAND FOR

Principles that close the loop.

Four commitments behind every agent we ship.

01

Outcomes over output

We measure agents by revenue saved and hours returned — not by messages sent. If it doesn't move your number, it doesn't ship.

02

Verified autonomy

Autonomy without accountability is a liability. Every agent action is logged, observable, replayable and reversible.

03

Humans in command

Agents absorb the repetitive; people keep the judgment. Escalation to a human is a feature we engineer, never an afterthought.

04

Security by default

ISO 27001 and SOC 2 aligned controls, GDPR and DPDP compliance, encryption everywhere. Trust is the platform.

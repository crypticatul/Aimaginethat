THE AGENTIC LOOP

Not chatbots. Autonomous operators.

Every AImagineThat agent runs a continuous loop — sensing context, reasoning over goals, executing across your tools, and verifying every outcome before moving on.

  1. 01

    Perceive

    Ingests calls, messages, CRM records, webhooks and documents in real time — across every channel.

  2. 02

    Plan

    Reasons over goals and guardrails to break the mission into concrete, ordered actions.

  3. 03

    Act

    Executes — makes the call, sends the quote, books the meeting, updates the pipeline.

  4. 04

    Verify

    Checks outcomes against intent. Retries, escalates to humans, or closes the loop.

  5. 05

    Learn

    Every interaction sharpens the playbook — objections, timing, channel mix, tone.

FAQ

Questions, answered.

What is agentic AI automation?

Agentic AI automation combines autonomous AI agents — systems that perceive context, plan multi-step actions, execute across your tools, and verify outcomes — with end-to-end workflow automation. Entire business processes run without manual intervention.

How fast can we deploy our first agent?

Most teams launch a production agent in under a week. Pre-built playbooks for sales, support and operations cut setup to hours, and our solutions engineers guide the rollout end-to-end.

Which channels and tools do agents work across?

Voice (PSTN & web), WhatsApp, Instagram, Messenger, LinkedIn, email and SMS — plus native integrations with HubSpot, Salesforce, telephony providers, calendars, and 200+ apps via API and webhooks.

Is our data secure?

Yes. ISO 27001 and SOC 2 aligned controls, GDPR and DPDP compliance, encryption in transit and at rest, role-based access, and configurable data residency.

Do AI agents replace my team?

No — they absorb the repetitive 80% so your team focuses on judgment work. Agents hand off to humans instantly when conversations get complex, with full context transferred in under two seconds.

What does implementation look like?

Week 1: discovery + agent design. Week 2: sandbox with your knowledge base and tools. Week 3: supervised production with live monitoring. Then full autonomy with human-in-the-loop guardrails you control.

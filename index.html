FAQ

Questions, answered.

What is an Agentic AI CRM?

An Agentic AI CRM combines core CRM records (deals, leads, contacts, quotes, tasks) with AI assistants that understand record context, recommend next actions, and trigger automation safely with approval controls.

How fast can we launch the CRM?

Most teams go live in days. We configure pipelines, import contacts, map your process, and activate AI-assisted workflows quickly without long consulting cycles.

Which CRM features are included?

Deals pipeline, leads board, contacts and companies, quotations, tasks, payment tracking, workflow automation, report builder, API/webhooks, and AI assist features like Deal Coach and Smart Compose.

Is our data secure?

Yes. ISO 27001 and SOC 2 aligned controls, GDPR and DPDP compliance, encryption in transit and at rest, role-based access, and configurable data residency.

Do AI agents replace my team?

No — they absorb the repetitive 80% so your team focuses on judgment work. Agents hand off to humans instantly when conversations get complex, with full context transferred in under two seconds.

What does implementation look like?

Week 1: discovery + agent design. Week 2: sandbox with your knowledge base and tools. Week 3: supervised production with live monitoring. Then full autonomy with human-in-the-loop guardrails you control.
